The map covers three platforms. Native apps are described once for Android and iPhone together. Websites are described once with support for 7 browsers.

## Capabilities per kind

| Kind | What it means | Android and iPhone | Web |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Android

Android pages show the permission string, the protection level (`normal`, `dangerous`, `signature` and others), the API level where it first appeared, how access is granted, what the user sees, how long it lasts, and where to take it back in Settings. Of the 361 native capabilities, 239 are fully supported on Android, 50 are partial, 20 are behind a flag or preference, 39 are not available and 13 were removed.

<Callout title="Android and iPhone share one page">
Each native page has one side by side table. Status, first version, permission, protection, grant, prompt, persistence, revocation, store policy and API appear in an Android column and an iPhone column.
</Callout>

## iPhone

iPhone pages show the Info.plist key or entitlement, whether the user is prompted, how long the choice lasts, Settings paths and App Store rules. Of the 361 native capabilities, 209 are fully supported on iPhone, 56 are partial, 40 are behind a flag or preference, 53 are not available and 3 were removed.
