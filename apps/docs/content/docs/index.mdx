title: Platform.Ontology
description: An open, plain language map of what apps and websites can do on your phone and in your browser.
---

Platform.Ontology is an open map of **844 capabilities**: the things an app or a website can do, get, or ask for on a phone or in a browser. Each one has a plain language page that says what it is, what it can and cannot reach, what the user sees, and how Android, iPhone and the web differ.

- **361 native app capabilities** cover Android and iPhone side by side on one page, so you see the same idea on both systems without reading two lists.
- **483 web capabilities** cover 7 browsers: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android and Samsung Internet.
- **24 groups** sort them, from location and camera to payments, tracking and on-device AI.
- **7 kinds** say how each one behaves: asks first, you pick, works without asking, does something, gets silently, used to track you, or blocked.

<Callout title="How to read a capability page">
Start with the one line summary and the reach meter. The meter runs from 1 (one thing, one moment) to 4 (broad, anytime). Then read what it can and cannot do, and open the platform table for the exact permission names, prompts and first versions.
</Callout>
