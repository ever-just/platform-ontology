Do Not Disturb access

Lets the app read and change Do Not Disturb settings.

Asks you firstSystem controlFocusAndroid and iPhoneWhile you use it

In one line

Can check and change Do Not Disturb on Android; iPhone shows only a yes or no

Lets the app read and change Do Not Disturb settings.

Reveals whether the user is in a focus mode and lets apps toggle it. iOS shows only a yes or no focus status.

What it can and can't do

Reach 2 of 4Your quiet mode, while allowed

What the user does: You switch on Do Not Disturb access in Settings (Android), or tap Share Focus Status (iPhone)

It can

  • Check whether Do Not Disturb is on
  • Change the interruption filter on Android
  • Learn whether a Focus is on, as a yes or no (iPhone)

It cannot

  • Change Focus on iPhone
  • Learn the Focus name or schedule on iPhone
  • Change Do Not Disturb on Android without the Settings switch

How it differs between platforms: Android lets an allowed app change Do Not Disturb; iPhone gives apps only a yes or no and no control.

The story

What it is

Lets an app read and change your Do Not Disturb settings.

For you

A special switch asks to let the app control Do Not Disturb. If you allow it, a meeting app can silence your phone for you. A bedtime app can turn it on at night.

For makers

Apps that care about sleep, meetings and focus want to flip the setting for you. Reading it also tells them if you are busy. On iPhone it is only a yes or no.

Risks

  • Shows if you are busy
  • App could silence alarms
  • Sleep schedule revealed

Android and iPhone in short

  • Android: Android needs special access to change the interruption filter.
  • iPhone: iPhone only returns a yes or no for whether a Focus is on and cannot change it.
