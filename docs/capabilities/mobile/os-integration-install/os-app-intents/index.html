App actions for assistants (App Intents, AppFunctions)

Lets Siri, Shortcuts or Gemini run actions inside an app.

Works without askingHome screen and system linksAssistant hooksAndroid and iPhoneIn the background

In one line

Can let Siri, Shortcuts or Gemini run actions inside the app, with your confirmation

Lets Siri, Shortcuts or Gemini run actions inside an app.

An app exposes verbs and data to the system assistant, so a voice request can act without opening the app. Modern assistants can chain these across apps.

What it can and can't do

Reach 2 of 4Only actions you ask for

What the user does: You ask Siri or Gemini to do something, or tap an action in Shortcuts or Spotlight

It can

  • Run an action in the app by voice or a shortcut
  • Show actions in Spotlight and on the Action button on iPhone
  • Chain actions together on iPhone with Apple Intelligence

It cannot

  • Run sensitive actions without asking you first
  • Let any app call another app's actions on Android; callers are limited to privileged assistants
  • Take actions you have not allowed in the Siri or Shortcuts settings

How it differs between platforms: iPhone uses App Intents. Android 16 uses AppFunctions, limited to trusted assistants.

The story

What it is

Lets Siri, Shortcuts or Gemini do tasks inside an app without opening it.

For you

You say add milk to my list or play my workout playlist and it happens without opening the app. You may be asked to confirm sensitive steps.

For makers

Makers get their features inside assistants and search. Modern assistants can chain actions across apps, which raises the risk of tricks hidden in content an assistant reads.

Risks

  • Prompt injection through agent chains
  • Actions without opening the app

Android and iPhone in short

  • Android: AppFunctions arrive with Android 16 and work only with trusted assistants.
  • iPhone: iOS 18 and 26 let Siri and Apple Intelligence chain and understand app content.

What the platform says

  • Apple: App Intents. Apple lets apps expose actions and content to Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight.
  • Google: AppFunctions. Google describes AppFunctions as a way for AI assistants to call actions in apps on Android 16.

Good uses and abuse

Legitimate uses

  • Voice control
  • Cross app automation

Known abuse

  • Prompt injection through agent chains
