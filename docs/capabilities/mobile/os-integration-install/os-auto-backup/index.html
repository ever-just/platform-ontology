Android Auto Backup and restore

Android copies up to 25 MB of app data to Google Drive about once a day and restores it at the next install.

Works without askingHome screen and system linksBackupAndroid and iPhoneIn the backgroundAfter you uninstall

In one line

Can save up to 25 MB of app data to your Google account daily

Android copies up to 25 MB of app data to Google Drive about once a day and restores it at the next install.

A reinstall or new phone can bring old app data back, including identifiers the developer stored. Android 16 QPR2 adds transfer of some data to iOS.

What it can and can't do

Reach 3 of 4A daily copy of app data

What the user does: Nothing. It happens when Android backup is on and the phone is idle

It can

  • Back up app settings and data, up to 25 MB, roughly every 24 hours
  • Restore that data when you reinstall or set up a new phone
  • Restore before the first launch of the app

It cannot

  • Back up more than 25 MB
  • Back up when you turned off Settings, Google, Backup
  • Keep backups for a phone you stopped using; they are deleted after 57 days
  • Do this on iPhone, which uses iCloud Backup instead

How it differs between platforms: Android has Auto Backup with a 25 MB cap. iPhone backs up the whole app folder with iCloud Backup.

The story

What it is

Android quietly copies up to 25 MB of app data to Google Drive and restores it on reinstall.

For you

You get a new phone and your app settings and progress are already there. You never press backup. The same thing happens after a reinstall.

For makers

Makers get restores for free. The downside is that stored identifiers can also return, so one person can look like the same user across reinstalls.

Risks

  • Identifiers carried across reinstall
  • Old data returns unseen

Android and iPhone in short

  • Android: About 25 MB per app, once about every 24 hours, restored at install before first launch.
  • iPhone: iOS uses iCloud Backup of the whole app container instead.

What the platform says

  • Google: Auto Backup for apps. Google describes Auto Backup as a way for apps to save data to Drive so it returns after a reinstall or a new phone.

Good uses and abuse

Legitimate uses

  • Restore settings
  • Move to new phone

Known abuse

  • Carrying identifiers across reinstall
