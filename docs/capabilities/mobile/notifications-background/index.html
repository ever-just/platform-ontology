CapabilityKindIn one line

App Standby BucketsWorks without askingCan't run as much in the background if you rarely open the app
Auto-granted exact alarmsAsks you firstCan set exact alarms by itself on Android, if it is an alarm or calendar app
Background audio and media playbackWorks without askingCan keep playing sound with the screen off, with new limits in Android 17
Background downloads and uploadsWorks without askingCan finish downloads and uploads for the app even after you close it
Background start restrictionsWorks without askingCan't freely start work or open screens from the background
Bluetooth Low Energy in the backgroundWorks without askingCan keep talking to Bluetooth gadgets with the app closed, with strict scan rules
Communication notifications and conversationsWorks without askingCan show who a message is from, with their photo, in the alert
Critical alertsWorks without askingCan ring through Do Not Disturb, Focus and the mute switch, if Apple approves the app
Deferrable background work (WorkManager, JobScheduler)Works without askingCan run background jobs on Android, but not more often than every 15 minutes
Doze and idle modeWorks without askingCan't force the phone to stay awake; Doze delays jobs, alarms and network
Expedited and user-initiated long jobsWorks without askingCan run jobs soon or for a long time after you tap something, with daily limits
FCM message delivery: payload, TTL, collapseWorks without askingCan send alerts to an Android phone through Google, up to 4 KB per message
FCM priority and deprioritizationWorks without askingCan wake a sleeping Android phone with a high priority push, but misuse gets slowed down

Foreground serviceWorks without askingCan run long tasks on Android with a visible notification, but only for a stated purpose

Foreground service types (Android 14)Works without askingCan run long Android background tasks, but only for a declared purpose with a notification
Full screen intentAsks you firstCan take over your Android screen for a call or alarm, only if you allow it
Ignore battery optimizationAsks you firstCan ask to keep running in the background on Android, only if you agree
iOS app refresh and processing tasksWorks without askingCan ask iPhone for short refreshes of about 30 seconds, but the system picks the time
iOS background modesWorks without askingCan run in the background on iPhone, but only for the kind of job Apple approved
Live Activities and Dynamic IslandWorks without askingCan pin live status to your iPhone lock screen for up to 8 hours
Live Activity push updates and budgetWorks without askingCan update a Live Activity by push, but important updates count against an hourly budget
Live Updates and Live ActivitiesAsks you firstCan show a live status on your lock screen, if you allow it for the app
Low Power Mode, Battery Saver and Background App Refresh stateGets silentlyCan see if your phone is saving power, and slow itself down to match
Notification channels and categoriesWorks without askingCan sort its alerts into types you can silence one by one
Notification service extension and mutable contentWorks without askingCan edit an iPhone push for about 30 seconds before you see it
Notification summary, grouping and cooldownGets silentlyCan't decide alone when your alerts arrive; the system groups them and holds some back
NotificationsAsks you firstCan show alerts, banners and badges, but only after you allow it
OEM background killersBlocked for appsCan't count on running in the background on some Android phones, since brands add extra killers
Ongoing and foreground service notificationsWorks without askingCan show a notification you cannot easily swipe away while background work runs
Provisional notification deliveryAsks you firstCan send quiet notifications without asking first, which you can keep or turn off
Push to TalkWorks without askingCan start walkie talkie voice from the background on iPhone, with microphone consent
Push token registration (FCM token, APNs device token)Works without askingCan get an address so its server can reach your app, even when closed
Region monitoring and geofencesWorks without askingCan wake the app when you enter or leave set places, up to 20 on iPhone
Restricted bucket and battery restriction settingsWorks without askingCan only run in the background as much as your battery setting allows
Rich notification media attachmentsWorks without askingCan attach pictures, sound or video to an iPhone alert, within size limits
Run at startupWorks without askingCan start itself in the background after your Android phone restarts
Significant location changeWorks without askingCan learn that you moved a long way, about 500 meters, even with the app closed
Silent (background) pushWorks without askingCan wake the app quietly with a push, but only a few times an hour
Time Sensitive and DND-bypass alertsWorks without askingCan mark an alert urgent so it breaks through Focus for about an hour
Uninstall detection through push failuresUsed to track youCan find out the app was removed, but only after hours or days
Visit monitoringWorks without askingCan learn which places you stay at, with arrival and leave times, on iPhone
VoIP push (PushKit) and call-style wakeupsWorks without askingCan wake the app right away for a call, if it shows a call screen
Wake lockDoes something on your deviceCan keep your phone's processor awake on Android, or the screen on iPhone
