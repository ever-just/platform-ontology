Body sensors

Asks to read heart rate and similar body sensor data while the app is in use.

Asks you firstHealth and fitnessVitalsAndroid and iPhoneWhile you use it

In one line

Can see your heart rate and similar body signals while the app is open

Asks to read heart rate and similar body sensor data while the app is in use.

Heart rate and related vitals reveal fitness, stress and health conditions. Android 16 replaces this permission with granular health permissions for apps targeting 36.

What it can and can't do

Reach 2 of 4While the app is open

What the user does: You tap Start workout or Measure heart rate and then Allow

It can

  • See live heart rate from the phone or a connected watch while the app is open
  • Choose While using the app or Only this time on Android 11 and newer
  • Ask for per type access in the Health sheet on iPhone
  • Show your readings during a workout

It cannot

  • See your heart rate when the app is closed, without the extra background permission
  • Use the old body sensors permission in apps aimed at Android 16; granular health permissions replace it
  • Tell on iPhone if you denied access; it just looks like no data
  • Keep going if you turn it off in Settings

How it differs between platforms: Android 16 replaces this with finer health permissions and a privacy policy step. iPhone uses the Health sheet with per type switches.

The story

What it is

Lets an app read your heart rate and similar body readings while you use it.

For you

A pop up asks to access body sensors. If you allow it, the app can read your heart rate and similar vitals while you use it. It can show stress or health problems, so say yes only for a good reason.

For makers

Fitness and wellness apps use it to show live heart rate and zones. Android 16 replaces this old permission with narrower health permissions for apps that target it.

Risks

  • Reveals health and stress
  • Data can be sold or leaked

Android and iPhone in short

  • Android: Uses the BODY_SENSORS permission, replaced by granular health permissions on Android 16.
  • iPhone: iPhone apps read heart rate through HealthKit permissions, per data type.

What the platform says

  • Google: Health Connect. Developer pitch: one place for health and fitness data with user control over which apps can read or write it.
