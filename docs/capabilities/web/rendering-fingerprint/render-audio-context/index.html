Audio fingerprint (OfflineAudioContext)

Renders a sound offline and reads tiny numeric differences.

Gets silentlyGraphics and fingerprintingRenderingWeb browsersWhen the page loadsWhile you use it

In one line

Can make a silent test sound to tell your device apart from others

Renders a sound offline and reads tiny numeric differences.

Audio stack differences give a device specific value without ever playing sound or asking permission. LinkedIn uses OfflineAudioContext(1,44100,44100).

What it can and can't do

Reach 2 of 4Silent, while the page runs

What the user does: Nothing. It happens when the page loads

It can

  • Process a short sound in memory without playing it
  • Measure tiny number differences that depend on your device and browser
  • Combine that number with other signals to recognise you later

It cannot

  • Play sound through your speakers
  • Record from your microphone
  • Ask you first, since there is no pop up
  • Work as an ID on its own, because many similar devices give the same result

The story

What it is

A page plays a sound silently in memory and reads tiny number differences that vary by device.

For you

You hear nothing and see no prompt. The page just does some math that a sound chip would do, and the answer helps it recognize your device later.

For makers

Trackers and fraud teams add this to a bundle of signals to build a device ID. It is cheap, needs no permission, and is hard for people to notice. The real feature exists so audio apps can process sound offline.

Risks

  • Hard to reset
  • Browsers are adding noise to fight it
