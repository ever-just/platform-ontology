In one line

Can't see your whole contact list, call history or text messages

A site cannot read your whole contact list, call history or SMS.

The only contact route is the picker, one chosen entry at a time. No web API reads call logs, SMS, voicemail or calendar events.

What it can and can't do

Reach 1 of 4Blocked in every browser

What the user does: Nothing. No browser lets a site do this, even if you tap Allow

It can

  • Ask you to pick contacts one at a time, on browsers that offer a contact picker
  • Receive only the name, number or email you chose in the picker

It cannot

  • Browse your full address book
  • See who you called or who called you
  • Look at your text messages or voicemail
  • See your calendar events

How it differs between platforms: The contact picker exists only in Chrome on Android. Safari, Firefox and desktop browsers have no way to share contacts at all.

Technical limits

  • No bulk contact read
  • No SMS send or read (sms: links open the user's app only)
  • No call log
  • No calendar read (only .ics download or a linked calendar via OAuth)

The story

What it is

A website cannot read your full contact list, call history, texts or calendar. The browser blocks it by design.

For you

You never see a prompt for this, because the option does not exist. If a site wants a contact, you pick the person yourself from a list your phone shows, and the site gets only that one entry.

For makers

Makers who want a friends list, a call log or message history cannot get it from a web page. They either build a native app, use a sign in with Google or Microsoft that shares contacts, or ask people to type or upload names.

Risks

  • Native apps can ask for much more
  • Phishing can still ask you to type contacts

What the platform says

  • Google Chrome: Contact Picker API. Lets a site ask for one or a few contacts you choose by hand, instead of your whole address book.

Good uses and abuse

Legitimate uses

  • None (by design)

Known abuse

  • Apps that want these must be native or use OAuth APIs
